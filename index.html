The premise

What happens when the story is entirely yours to write? No experience needed. Each game tells you what to do next, one small step at a time.

Option one

Play on screen

Type your answers straight into the game. It saves as you go, and at the end you can download your story.

Option two

Play on paper

Print the play kit and write by hand. The game still gives you every prompt, example and bit of help, just without the typing boxes.

How it works

Three steps, and you can stop and come back whenever you like.

01

Pick a game

Each one gives you a different thing to be, and a different story to tell.

02

Take the tutorial

A three minute practice turn shows you everything. Skip it if you like.

03

Write your story

Answer short prompts, make a change, and see where it takes you. Stuck? Roll a die.
